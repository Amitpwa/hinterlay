## Full description

```
Hinterlay puts your text behind the person, pet or object in your photo, the depth effect you see on magazine covers and viral posts. It finds the subject right on your phone, so your photos never leave your device.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a photo or take one with the built-in camera.
2. Type your words. Hinterlay detects the subject and slides the text behind it.
3. Style it, then save or share.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Text behind anything: people, pets, cars, buildings, food
• Do magic: the app reads your scene and suggests complete text styles. Tap one to use it, or ask for more ideas
• Lots of fonts, glossy bubble type, shadows with strength and colour control, and letter case options
• Perspective warp to match the angle of your scene
• Erase brush: hide just part of a word, or remove part of the subject
• Crop, photo adjustments and skin smoothing
• Day, date and place stamps for your photos
• Camera with live filters and face masks
• Turn any creation into a WhatsApp sticker
• 3D tilt live wallpaper that moves as you tilt your phone
• Library of everything you have made, with a simple Inspire tab for ideas
• Home screen widgets, app shortcuts and a quick camera from the lock screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Subject detection and the AI suggestions run on your phone
• No account, no sign-up
• Your photos and creations are stored only on your device
• Anonymous usage statistics are optional and can be turned off any time in More
• Analytics are never recorded on the editor or camera screens

FREE, WITH OPTIONS
Hinterlay is free and shows ads. Prefer none? Go ad-free for life with a one-time purchase, or leave an optional tip to support a solo developer. Tips don't unlock anything.

Questions or ideas? Write to ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in

Privacy policy: https://amitpwa.github.io/hinterlay-privacy/
```
