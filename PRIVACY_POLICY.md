# Privacy policy

The published policy lives in [`privacy-site/index.md`](privacy-site/index.md) (upload that folder to a public GitHub repo and turn on Pages).
The in-app copy is `app/src/main/assets/privacy_policy.md`. Keep the two in sync and bump `PRIVACY_POLICY_VERSION` in `PrivacyConsent.kt` when the meaning changes, so users are asked to accept again.
