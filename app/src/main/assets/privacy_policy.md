# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Publisher: Ashutosh Pandey

Contact: ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in

## The short version
- Your photos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded, unless you choose to send one creation with Share with the world (section 9).
- No account or login.
- We receive anonymous usage counts only, and you can switch that off.
- Ads come from Google AdMob and you choose your ad consent.
- Payments are handled by Google Play.

## 1. Your photos stay on your device
Hinterlay edits photos you choose with Android's photo picker, or take with the in-app camera. Subject detection, text placement, erasing, cropping and skin smoothing run on your phone using Google ML Kit. We do not upload, store on a server, or share your photos. Exported images are saved to your gallery (Pictures/Hinterlay) and shared only when you choose to share them.

## 2. Camera and location
- Camera: used only while the camera screen is open, to take a photo you choose to edit. Face masks use on-device face detection; faces are not stored, identified or uploaded.
- Approximate location: used only if you tap the Place stamp, to print your city or area on a photo. Android's built-in geocoder turns your coordinates into a place name. We do not save or receive your location.
- Both permissions are optional.

## 3. Saved creations
Your history (edited photos, text settings and previews) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Delete any item from Library, or clear the app's data, to remove it. Nothing is synced to us.

## 4. Ads
Hinterlay is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), app interaction data and diagnostic information to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.
- In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws you are asked for your choices through Google's consent tool before ads load.
- Change them any time in More, Ad choices.
- Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.

## 5. Usage data and analytics
- Google Firebase and Google Analytics record a random install identifier, app version, Android version, language, which screens are opened and counts of actions such as exports. No photos, typed text, names, emails or phone numbers.
- Microsoft Clarity records how menus and browsing screens are used (taps, scrolls, layout). It is switched off on every screen that can show your photos: the editor, crop and camera.
- Both follow one switch: More, Share anonymous usage data.

## 6. Notifications and content
If you allow notifications, you receive occasional tips and updates through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Turn them off in More, Tips and updates, or in Android settings. Inspiration images and announcements are downloaded from our server.

## 7. On-device AI and effects
Caption ideas, font suggestions and colours (Do magic), face masks (on-device face mesh), cartoon, comic, pop-art and pencil stickers and every photo effect are produced on your phone from the photo you open, using Google ML Kit and the app's own code. The photo never leaves your device and no extra models are downloaded for these features.

## 8. Purchases and support
Tips and the lifetime ad-free option are processed by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase happened. If a PayPal donation link is shown, it opens PayPal in your browser; PayPal handles that payment under its own policy, and a donation unlocks nothing in the app.

## 9. Sharing and invites
When you share a creation, the share sheet is handled by Android and the app you pick. Your invite code is a random code stored on your device. It does not identify you.

Share with the world (optional): in Inspire you can send one creation of your choice to us for review so it can be featured. It is the only way a photo of yours reaches us, and nothing is sent unless you pick it, tick that it is yours to share and that we may feature it, and tap Send for review.
- Sent: the one exported image, its text style, an optional line and credit name, and your email only if you tick the optional box about the creator programme. An anonymous sign-in identifier is created for the entry.
- Used: to review it, to feature it with your credit if it passes, and, if you agreed, to email you about the creator programme. Not sold, not used for advertising.
- Kept on Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage). Rejected entries are deleted.
- Ask us any time to remove a submitted or featured creation and your email: ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in
- Sending starts only after an app update that turns it on and asks you to accept this policy again.

## 10. Who receives data
We do not sell your data. Only these services receive information: Google (AdMob, Firebase, Analytics, Play), Microsoft (Clarity) and PayPal if you use the optional donate link. Each follows its own privacy policy.

## 11. Your choices and rights
- Turn off usage data: More, Share anonymous usage data.
- Change ad consent: More, Ad choices.
- Delete everything stored by the app: uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings.
- Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold only anonymous data, but you are welcome to ask: ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in

## 12. Retention and security
Anonymous usage data is kept for the analytics providers' standard retention periods, then deleted. Data in transit is encrypted. Photos and creations never leave your device.

## 13. Children
Hinterlay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 14. Changes
If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and ask you to accept it again.
